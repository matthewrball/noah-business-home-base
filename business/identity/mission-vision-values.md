# Mission, vision, and values

**Draft 0 — these are proposals for reaction, not adopted statements.** Noah's site describes Ark as a second-layer protocol for Bitcoin and presents Noah as its gateway; its claims emphasize simple onboarding, working with Lightning, and control [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/]. The design goals say to "keep things simple on the surface, but reward curiosity" [src: https://gist.github.com/hsjoberg/8196abaa9ef365f25038f9130b585559].

## Proposal A — approachable Bitcoin gateway

**Mission — 16 words:** Make Bitcoin payments approachable through Ark, with a clear path from first use to deeper understanding [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/] [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa].

**Vision — 27 words:** Anyone can receive and send Bitcoin with a welcoming mobile wallet, learn only as much as they choose, and understand the system when curiosity takes them deeper [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/] [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa].

Inputs: newcomer audience, simple surface, and optional technical depth [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa].

## Proposal B — simplest Ark wallet

**Mission — 9 words:** Build the simplest mobile gateway to Bitcoin's Ark protocol [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/] [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa].

**Vision — 25 words:** Noah becomes the everyday gateway to Ark: simple on the surface, working with Lightning, and open to anyone who wants to understand what happens underneath [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/] [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa].

Inputs: the existing gateway language, working with Lightning, and the gist's simplicity principle [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/] [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa].

## Proposal C — lessons into a calmer wallet

**Mission — 11 words:** Turn hard-won wallet lessons into a calm, open, curiosity-rewarding Bitcoin experience [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa] [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/].

**Vision — 27 words:** Bitcoin's newest ways to pay feel as familiar as a modern wallet, while open-source builders and curious users can inspect, improve, and learn from the product together [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa] [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/].

Inputs: Blixt's documented maintenance lesson, modern-wallet references, and Noah's open-source status [src: gist hsjoberg/8196abaa] [src: https://beta.noahwallet.io/].
